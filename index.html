Read this before anything else

From here on out, your trip is yours

Everything past this page is a suggestion, not a schedule. There are a couple of birthday wishes and a few things already on the books, but nobody is obligated to a single one of them.

These two days are for fun and magic. If the group is off doing something you have no interest in, go do the thing you actually want to do instead. Sleep in. Split off. Ride the same ride four times in a row. Take the babies back to the hotel in the afternoon. Nobody is counting heads and nobody is going to be offended.

We do not all have to stay together to have a good trip. Plan around what you love, not around what everybody else happened to pick.

The one thing I do ask

If you are not joining the lunch reservation, tell me ahead of time, not that morning. The seats are booked and held under our name, and empty chairs still get charged.

Glad you made it, friend.

Two things, and that’s the whole birthday wish

Sunday the 20th is my birthday. These two are the entire ask, and past them you are free.

  1. A picture in front of the castle, all of us, Sunday morning. Sunday the 20th, time, at the castle. Every last one of us in one frame. That is the one photo I want out of this whole trip, and it only works if nobody is off riding something.
  2. Lunch together at Cafe Orleans, same day. 12:20pm, New Orleans Square. Sixteen of us had to go in as three reservations, so please be at the podium at 12:20 even if your seating time says later. They cannot hold tables for people who have not shown up yet.

The fine print, from one traveler to another

  • Indiana Jones is closed. Went down September 8 with no reopening date announced. Do not plan a morning around it.
  • Haunted Mansion is wearing its Halloween costume. Nightmare Before Christmas overlay, running all fall. Same ride, completely different, and a much longer line than usual. Ride it early or not at all.
  • Sunday the 20th, California Adventure closes to us at 6pm. There is a hard-ticket Halloween party that night and we do not have tickets. Anything you want over there, do it Saturday.
  • There is no early entry anymore. Disneyland ended it in January. Nobody gets in before opening, so the only real edge is being at the turnstiles early.
  • Everybody has a Park Hopper. Move between the two parks any time of day. The old 11am rule is gone.
  • Kids under 3 are free. No ticket, no dining charge, no wristband.
  • Download the Disneyland app before you fly. Wait times, mobile food ordering, and the park map all live in there, and the wifi at the gate is miserable.
