At night we are watching Fantasmic on the Rivers of America. We did not buy the dining package, so a couple of us will go hold ground early and the rest can wander over whenever. Come if you want it, skip it if the kids are done.

Before you pack

The fine print, from one traveler to another

  • Indiana Jones is closed. Went down September 8 with no reopening date announced. Do not plan a morning around it.
  • Haunted Mansion is wearing its Halloween costume. Nightmare Before Christmas overlay, running all fall. Same ride, completely different, and a much longer line than usual. Ride it early or not at all.
  • Sunday the 20th, California Adventure closes to us at 6pm. There is a hard-ticket Halloween party that night and we do not have tickets. Anything you want over there, do it Saturday.
  • There is no early entry anymore. Disneyland ended it in January. Nobody gets in before 8:00, so the only real edge is being at the turnstiles early.
  • Everybody has a Park Hopper. Move between the two parks any time of day. The old 11am rule is gone.
  • Kids under 3 are free. No ticket, no dining charge, no wristband.
  • Download the Disneyland app before you fly. Wait times, mobile food ordering, and the park map all live in there, and the wifi at the gate is miserable.
